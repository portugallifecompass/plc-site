About Portugal Life Compass

RULES AS OF JULY 2026

Portugal Life Compass exists for one reason: most of what you read online about moving to Portugal — taxes, visas, property, pensions, crypto — is either outdated, oversimplified, or simply wrong. We make short, calm, carefully verified videos and guides that treat you like an adult making a serious financial decision.

Who this is for

Remote professionals weighing a move, retirees planning life on a foreign pension, and investors or entrepreneurs who need to understand how Portugal actually taxes them. Everything is in plain English, built for an international audience.

How we verify — our editorial rule

Every legal or tax statement we publish is checked against official sources only before it ships:

  • Diário da República — Portugal's official gazette, consolidated legislation;
  • Portal das Finanças — the Portuguese Tax Authority;
  • AIMA and official government portals — for immigration and residency;
  • EUR-Lex — for EU law.

If a claim cannot be verified in those sources, it does not get published — we say so, or we leave it out. We never cite "probable" article numbers or second-hand summaries. Each episode is built on a written verification dossier, and every page and video carries the date its rules were checked ("Rules as of July 2026"). When the law changes — and in Portugal it changes every year with the State Budget — we update.

How we work

The channel publishes a new episode every Thursday, with short versions on YouTube Shorts. Research, scripting and production make intensive use of AI tools under human editorial control; verification against official sources is the step that never gets automated away.

Portugal Life Compass provides general information, not tax, legal, immigration or financial advice. Your situation is unique — always confirm it with a qualified professional or the competent Portuguese authority. See the full disclaimer.
