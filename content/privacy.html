Privacy Policy

LAST UPDATED: JULY 2026

Short version: this website is designed to know as little about you as possible.

What we collect

Nothing directly. This site has no user accounts, no comment system, no newsletter sign-up and no contact forms at this time. We do not set cookies, and we do not run advertising or cross-site tracking.

Hosting and technical logs

The site is served by Cloudflare (Cloudflare, Inc.), our hosting and content-delivery provider. Like virtually all web infrastructure, Cloudflare processes technical data about requests (such as IP address, browser type and pages requested) to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. This processing is covered by Cloudflare's privacy policy. We do not receive or store personal profiles from this data.

Analytics

If we measure traffic, we use privacy-preserving, cookie-less aggregate analytics (page views and referrers, not individual profiles). No consent banner is required because no personal identifiers are stored on your device. If this ever changes, this policy — and the site's consent mechanics — will change first.

External services

Videos are hosted on YouTube (Google). Following a link to YouTube, or downloading files hosted on third-party services (such as Google Drive), takes you to those providers, whose own privacy policies apply. We embed third-party content on our pages only where noted, and prefer links over embedded trackers.

Your rights (GDPR)

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation you have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection regarding personal data concerning you. Since we do not collect or store personal data ourselves, requests concerning technical request logs should be addressed to the providers listed above. For any privacy question about this website, contact us via the YouTube channel. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority — in Portugal, the CNPD (Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados).

Changes

If the site gains features that touch personal data — for example a newsletter — this policy will be updated before those features go live, with the date above revised.
